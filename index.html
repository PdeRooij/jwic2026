What else is in the bag?

Magnet

Here in the Netherlands we have something called a "tegeltjeswijsheid" (translated literally: tile wisdom). A memorable, wise or witty saying that is sometimes printed on a tile to hang on or put into a wall. Usually these tiles are produced in a Delft Blue print (Dutch: Delfts blauw). This magnet resembles a tegeltjeswijsheid. It is based on Proverbs 26:20. Unfortunately it is not an authentic Delft Blue tile, but a magnet is more convenient and durable (and also a lot cheaper 😉).

In line with the scripture, some bags also contain a tealight (Dutch: waxinelicht). 'Wood' for the 🔥...

There's more?!

Yes! Some typical Dutch snacks:

  • Stroopwafel A heavenly sweet biscuit with syrup (stroop) filling. Could it be manna...?
  • Boterbabbelaars Literally: butter chatterers. Butterscotch candy.
  • Hopjes Sweets with a light coffee/caramel flavour.
  • Bedankhartjes Literally: thank you hearts. Tiny, recognizable sweets, commonly gifted after a special occasion.

Enjoy the snacks! Hopefully they didn't melt in the hot weather. 🥵
